## __CLAUSE 4  —  DELIVERY OPERATIONS AND SERVICE STANDARDS__

### __4\.1  Receiving and Accepting Delivery Assignments__

4\.1\.1  Delivery Assignments are offered to the Delivery Partner through the HIVAGO Delivery Partner App based on the Delivery Partner's location, availability status, and the Company's assignment algorithm\. The Delivery Partner has complete discretion to accept or decline any Delivery Assignment without penalty\.

4\.1\.2  Once a Delivery Assignment is accepted by the Delivery Partner, the Delivery Partner is expected to:

- Proceed promptly to the Restaurant Partner's location to collect the Order;
- Collect the complete, sealed Order package from the Restaurant Partner and verify the Order details against the Delivery Partner App display;
- Deliver the Order to the User's designated address as displayed on the Delivery Partner App;
- Confirm delivery completion on the Delivery Partner App upon handing the Order to the User or leaving it at the designated safe spot \(where authorised\)\.

4\.1\.3  Acceptance Withdrawal: Once a Delivery Assignment is accepted, the Delivery Partner should not abandon it without genuine cause\. Frequent mid\-assignment cancellations may adversely affect the Delivery Partner's acceptance rate and eligibility for Incentives, and in extreme cases may constitute grounds for Deactivation\.

### __4\.2  Service Standards__

While the Delivery Partner is not an employee and operates independently, the following minimum service standards are expected to protect Users' experience and maintain Platform integrity:

- Handle all food packages with reasonable care to prevent damage, spillage, or contamination;
- Maintain appropriate personal hygiene and presentable conduct during delivery;
- Communicate professionally and respectfully with Users and Restaurant Partner staff;
- Not consume, tamper with, or open any sealed food package;
- Not misrepresent delivery status on the Delivery Partner App — do not mark an order 'Delivered' before actual delivery;
- Adhere to applicable traffic laws and road safety requirements at all times;
- Not operate the vehicle under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or any substance that impairs judgment;
- Not use the Delivery Partner App while actively operating a vehicle\.

### __4\.3  No Live GPS Tracking Disclosure__

The Delivery Partner acknowledges that the HIVAGO Customer App does not currently provide Users with real\-time GPS\-based live order tracking\. The Delivery Partner's location data collected during active Delivery Assignments is used solely for internal dispatch management, fraud prevention, and delivery dispute resolution, and is not shared with Users in real time\.

### __4\.4  Tip Collection__

Users may offer a tip to the Delivery Partner through the Platform\. All tip amounts are collected by the Company through the payment gateway and disbursed to the Delivery Partner in full as part of the Settlement Cycle\. The Company does not deduct any portion of User tips\. Tips are not guaranteed and form no part of the base earnings calculation\.

The Company does not provide the Delivery Partner with any vehicle, fuel, mobile device, internet connectivity, delivery bag, uniform, or any other equipment\. The Delivery Partner is solely responsible for all costs associated with operating, maintaining, fuelling, and insuring their vehicle\.

## __CLAUSE 5  —  EARNINGS — DELIVERY FEE, SURGE, INCENTIVES, AND TIPS__

### __5\.1  Delivery Fee — Base Rate__

The Delivery Partner shall earn a base Delivery Fee for each successfully completed Delivery Assignment\. The current base Delivery Fee structure is set out in Schedule A to this Agreement\. The Company reserves the right to revise the base Delivery Fee structure with 7 \(seven\) days' prior notice communicated through the Delivery Partner App or registered mobile number\.

A Delivery Assignment is 'successfully completed' when the Delivery Partner marks the Order as delivered on the Delivery Partner App after physically handing the Order to the User or placing it at an authorised delivery spot\.

### __5\.2  Surge Fee__

In addition to the base Delivery Fee, the Delivery Partner may earn a Surge Fee during periods of high demand, peak hours, adverse weather conditions, or supply\-demand imbalances in a particular zone\. Surge pricing is determined algorithmically by the Platform\. The applicable Surge Fee, if any, is displayed on the Delivery Partner App prior to Assignment acceptance\.

Surge Fees are variable and not guaranteed for any period\. The Company provides no assurance of when or how frequently surge conditions will arise\.

### __5\.3  Incentives__

The Company may, at its sole discretion, offer performance\-based Incentives to Delivery Partners who achieve defined metrics within a specified period \(e\.g\., completing a minimum number of deliveries within a week, maintaining an acceptance rate above a threshold\)\. Incentive programmes are:

- Published on the Delivery Partner App with full terms and earning conditions;
- Subject to verification — the Company will audit delivery data before disbursing Incentives;
- Non\-guaranteed — the Company may modify or withdraw any Incentive programme at any time with reasonable notice;
- Non\-payable where earned through fraudulent activity, fake deliveries, or manipulation of the acceptance rate\.

### __5\.4  Tips__

Tips voluntarily offered by Users through the Platform are passed to the Delivery Partner in full via the Settlement Cycle\. Tips are not subject to any commission or deduction by the Company\. Tips are however subject to TDS under applicable income tax provisions if total earnings including tips cross applicable thresholds\.

### __5\.5  Earnings Transparency__

The Delivery Partner may view their completed assignments, earnings breakdown \(base fee, surge, incentives, tips\), and pending settlements on the Delivery Partner App's earnings dashboard\. Earnings data is updated in near\-real\-time following delivery confirmation\.

__Note on Earnings__

Earnings under this Agreement are not a salary or wage\. They are service fees earned per completed delivery assignment\.

The Company does not guarantee any minimum daily, weekly, or monthly earnings\.

Earnings will vary based on the number of assignments accepted and completed, zone demand, and incentive eligibility\.

### __8\.4  Tax Deduction at Source \(TDS\) — Section 194C__

__TDS — TAX DEDUCTED AT SOURCE__

The Company is required by Section 194C of the Income Tax Act, 1961 to deduct TDS from payments made to Delivery Partners\.

TDS is deducted at the applicable rate \(currently 1% for individuals/HUFs or 2% for others\) on amounts exceeding the prescribed threshold\.

The Company will issue Form 16A \(TDS certificate\) to the Delivery Partner after the end of each financial quarter in which TDS has been deducted\.

Delivery Partners with a valid PAN on record will have TDS deducted at the applicable lower rate\. Without a valid PAN, TDS will be deducted at the higher rate of 20% as required under Section 206AA of the Income Tax Act\.

TDS deducted by the Company will be deposited with the Income Tax Department and reflected in the Delivery Partner's Form 26AS\.

8\.5  The Delivery Partner acknowledges that TDS deducted under Section 194C is a statutory deduction and does not represent the Company's liability for the Delivery Partner's income tax obligations\. The Delivery Partner remains solely responsible for filing their income tax return and paying any balance tax due after credit of the TDS amount\.

8\.6  Payment Disputes: If the Delivery Partner believes there is an error in their settlement amount, they must raise a dispute through the Delivery Partner App's earnings section or by writing to partner@hivago\.in within 7 \(seven\) Business Days of the settlement date\. The Company will investigate and resolve valid disputes within 14 \(fourteen\) Business Days\.

### __9\.1  Grounds for Deactivation__

The Company may Deactivate \(temporarily or permanently\) a Delivery Partner's account on the following grounds:

__Deactivation Grounds__

__Immediate Permanent Deactivation \(without notice\)__

Theft of food, cash, or User property; physical assault or abuse of a User, Restaurant Partner staff, or Company employee; sexual harassment or misconduct; criminal conviction; submission of forged documents; confirmed fraud involving fake deliveries or earnings manipulation\.

Lapse of vehicle insurance or driving licence; failure to complete BGV re\-verification; sustained pattern of false delivery confirmations; repeated customer complaints of tampering, unprofessional behaviour, or misconduct\.

__Deactivation with 7\-Day Notice__

Persistent low delivery completion rate; persistent acceptance rate below the Company's defined minimum threshold; prolonged inactivity without notification; breach of confidentiality obligations\.

A Delivery Partner may voluntarily terminate this Agreement by giving 7 \(seven\) days' written notice\. All pending payments will be settled in the next available Settlement Cycle\.

- The Delivery Partner shall have the right to submit a written response within the notice period specified above;
- The Company will consider the Delivery Partner's response before making a final decision;
- The Company's decision will be communicated to the Delivery Partner in writing\.

- The Company's decision on appeal is final, subject to the Delivery Partner's rights before courts of competent jurisdiction\.

9\.4  Pending earnings accrued up to the date of Deactivation will be settled in the next Settlement Cycle following confirmation of the Deactivation, subject to any deductions for verified losses caused by the Delivery Partner's breach\.
