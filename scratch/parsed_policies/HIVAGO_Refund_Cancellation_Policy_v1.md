## __1\.  DEFINITIONS__

In this Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned below\. Capitalised terms not defined here carry the meanings given in the HIVAGO Terms and Conditions\.

__Definitions__

__"Order Confirmation"__

A written confirmation sent by HIVAGO to the User via the Platform, push notification, or SMS confirming the Order has been successfully placed\.

__"Restaurant Acceptance"__

The point in time at which a Restaurant Partner acknowledges and begins preparation of an Order on the HIVAGO Restaurant Dashboard\.

__"Cancellation Window"__

The 2 \(two\) minute period from the Order Confirmation timestamp during which a User may cancel a standard real\-time Order, provided Restaurant Acceptance has not yet occurred\.

__"Platform Fee"__

The non\-refundable convenience charge levied by HIVAGO per Order for access to its technology platform, irrespective of order outcome\.

__"Delivery Fee"__

The logistics charge levied per Order for last\-mile delivery from the Restaurant Partner to the User's address\.

__"Refund"__

A credit of an eligible amount back to the User's original payment instrument via PayU\. No cash or alternate\-account refunds are issued\.

__"Non\-Delivery"__

An Order that was not received by the User at the designated address for reasons attributable to the Delivery Partner, Restaurant Partner, or the Company\.

__"Pre\-Order / Scheduled Order"__

An Order placed for delivery at a specified future time slot\.

__"Business Day"__

Any day other than a Sunday or declared public holiday under Maharashtra or Central Government notification\.

## __2\.  PLATFORM FEE — ABSOLUTE NON\-REFUNDABILITY__

__PLATFORM FEE POLICY — NO EXCEPTIONS__

The Platform Fee is charged at the time an Order is confirmed and represents HIVAGO's earned consideration for providing marketplace technology, routing, and intermediary services\.

These services are rendered the moment an Order is placed — regardless of what happens subsequently\.

The Platform Fee is NON\-REFUNDABLE in ALL scenarios: User cancellation, restaurant cancellation, Force Majeure, non\-delivery, quality issues, or technical errors\.

The Platform Fee is clearly disclosed at the checkout screen prior to payment\. Placing an Order constitutes your acceptance of this term\.

2\.1  The Platform Fee represents HIVAGO's service charge for facilitating the marketplace transaction, maintaining technology infrastructure, processing the order to the Restaurant Partner, and coordinating Delivery Partner dispatch\. These services are fully rendered upon Order confirmation regardless of delivery outcome\.

2\.2  Disclosure at Checkout: The Platform Fee amount is displayed as a separate line item on the order summary and checkout screen prior to the User's final payment confirmation\. Proceeding to payment constitutes the User's informed and express agreement to the Platform Fee and its non\-refundable nature\.

## __3\.  USER\-INITIATED CANCELLATION__

### __3\.1  Standard Real\-Time Orders — 2\-Minute Cancellation Window__

A User may cancel a standard \(real\-time/immediate\) Order within 2 \(two\) minutes of the Order Confirmation timestamp, subject to the condition that Restaurant Acceptance has not yet occurred\. Cancellation within this window entitles the User to:

- A full refund of the food item value;
- A full refund of the Delivery Fee; and
- No refund of the Platform Fee \(non\-refundable in all circumstances\)\.

To cancel within the Cancellation Window:

- Tap 'Cancel Order' on the Order Status screen within the HIVAGO app; OR
- Contact HIVAGO customer support immediately through the in\-app Help section\.

If the Cancellation Window has elapsed or Restaurant Acceptance has occurred prior to the cancellation request, the rules in Clause 3\.2 below apply\.

### __3\.2  Cancellation After Restaurant Acceptance__

Once a Restaurant Partner has accepted and commenced food preparation — which may occur within seconds of Order Confirmation — the User's right to cancel is significantly restricted\. The following assessment framework applies:

__Preparation Stage at Cancellation__

__Refund Treatment__

Accepted but not yet started preparation

Full food value refund likely\. Delivery Fee refunded if no partner dispatched\. Platform Fee non\-refundable\.

Preparation commenced / partially complete

Partial refund at Company's discretion\. Restaurant has incurred ingredient/labour costs\. Platform Fee non\-refundable\.

Preparation complete / order dispatched

No refund available\. Cancellation not possible once dispatched\. Platform Fee non\-refundable\.

__Why Your Refund May Be Reduced or Denied After Restaurant Acceptance__

Restaurant Partners begin preparing food within minutes of accepting an order and incur real food, fuel, and labour costs\.

HIVAGO's settlement obligation to the Restaurant Partner is triggered upon order acceptance\. A late cancellation by the User does not cancel this obligation\.

To avoid this situation, please review your order carefully at the cart and checkout stages before confirming payment\.

### __3\.3  Pre\-Orders and Scheduled Orders — Cancellation Schedule__

For orders placed for a future time slot, the following cancellation schedule applies based on the time remaining before the scheduled delivery slot:

__Time Before Scheduled Slot__

__Refund Treatment__

More than 1 hour

Full refund of food value and Delivery Fee\. Platform Fee non\-refundable\.

Between 30 minutes and 1 hour

Partial refund subject to review\. Restaurant Partner will be contacted to confirm preparation status\.

Less than 30 minutes

No refund\. Restaurant preparation is likely underway\. Platform Fee non\-refundable\.

### __3\.4  Item Unavailability Discovered After Confirmation__

If, post\-Order Confirmation, a Restaurant Partner reports that one or more items are unavailable, the Company will:

- Notify the User immediately by push notification and in\-app message;
- Offer the option to: \(a\) accept a like\-for\-like substitution; \(b\) proceed with available items with a proportionate refund; or \(c\) cancel the full order for a refund of food value and Delivery Fee;
- If the User does not respond within 10 \(ten\) minutes of notification, the Company may cancel the Order and process a full refund of food value and Delivery Fee\.

In all item unavailability scenarios, the Platform Fee remains non\-refundable\.

## __4\.  CANCELLATION BY THE COMPANY OR RESTAURANT PARTNER__

### __4\.1  Company\-Initiated Cancellation__

The Company may cancel an Order at its sole discretion in the following circumstances, without limiting its rights under the Terms and Conditions:

- No Delivery Partner is available in the User's serviceable area within a reasonable dispatch window;
- The Restaurant Partner is unreachable or temporarily unable to fulfil orders;
- The User's delivery address is confirmed as outside the active delivery zone;
- A Force Majeure Event renders fulfilment impossible or unsafe;
- Suspected fraudulent activity associated with the Order, payment, or User account;
- A payment transaction failure or high chargeback risk identified post\-order confirmation;
- Technical system errors preventing accurate order processing or delivery routing\.

In all Company\-initiated cancellations \(except fraud\-related\), the User will receive a full refund of the food value and Delivery Fee within 5–7 Business Days\. The Platform Fee remains non\-refundable\.

Fraud\-related cancellations may result in account suspension and forfeiture of all amounts paid, pending investigation\.

### __4\.2  Restaurant Partner\-Initiated Cancellation__

A Restaurant Partner may cancel an accepted Order in exceptional circumstances \(e\.g\., sudden kitchen closure, complete ingredient unavailability\)\. In such cases:

- The User will be notified immediately via push notification;
- A full refund of food value and Delivery Fee will be processed within 5–7 Business Days;
- The Platform Fee is non\-refundable;
- Repeated Restaurant Partner cancellations will be monitored\. The Company may impose penalties on or suspend the Restaurant Partner from the Platform\.

__Consumer Rights — Company/Restaurant Cancellation__

Where HIVAGO or a Restaurant Partner cancels an Order you have paid for, you are entitled to a full refund of the food value and Delivery Fee under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019\.

If the Company fails to process this refund within stated timelines, you may contact the National Consumer Helpline \(1800\-11\-4000\) or approach the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Navi Mumbai / Thane\.

## __5\.  REFUNDS FOR DELIVERY\-RELATED ISSUES__

### __5\.1  Non\-Delivery__

A Non\-Delivery claim arises when the User reports the Order was not received despite the status showing 'Delivered'\. The following process and rules apply:

- Claim must be raised within 2 \(two\) hours of the Order being marked 'Delivered';
- The Company will review Delivery Partner records, delivery confirmation data, and any available evidence;
- If Non\-Delivery is confirmed as attributable to the Delivery Partner: full refund of food value and Delivery Fee will be processed;
- If investigation is inconclusive: the Company may offer a partial refund or appropriate goodwill credit at its discretion;
- Platform Fee is non\-refundable even in confirmed non\-delivery scenarios\.

__NON\-DELIVERY — WHEN REFUNDS DO NOT APPLY__

User provided an incorrect or incomplete delivery address: No refund\.

User was unreachable/unavailable at the delivery location after the Delivery Partner arrived and made reasonable attempts: No refund\.

User failed to collect order within a reasonable time after receiving a delivery arrival notification: No refund\.

Note: HIVAGO does not currently offer live GPS tracking\. Ensure push notifications are enabled on your device during the expected delivery window\.

### __5\.2  Significant Delivery Delay__

Delivery time estimates on HIVAGO are indicative only\. Delays caused by Restaurant Partner preparation time, traffic conditions, weather, or peak demand are not grounds for a mandatory refund\. However, where a delay is materially attributable to Delivery Partner default, the Company will:

- Investigate the timeline against available records;
- Offer a partial refund of the Delivery Fee or a goodwill gesture, at its discretion;
- HIVAGO does not guarantee any specific delivery time and this cannot be treated as a service level commitment\.

### __5\.3  Wrong Items Delivered__

If items delivered differ materially from what was ordered:

- Claim must be raised within 1 \(one\) hour of delivery;
- Photographic evidence of the received items must be submitted via the in\-app Help section or emailed to support@hivago\.in;
- Upon verification, the Company will process a refund for the incorrectly delivered item value, or arrange a replacement delivery at its discretion;
- If the entire order is confirmed as incorrect: full refund of food value and Delivery Fee will be considered;
- Platform Fee non\-refundable\.

### __5\.4  Missing Items__

If one or more confirmed Order items are absent from the delivery:

- Claim must be raised within 1 \(one\) hour of delivery;
- The Company will verify against Restaurant Partner packing records;
- If validated: refund equal to the value of the missing item\(s\) will be processed;
- High\-frequency missing item claims from one account will be subject to fraud screening\.

### __5\.5  Food Quality Complaints__

HIVAGO is a marketplace intermediary\. Food quality, safety, temperature maintenance, and packaging are the exclusive responsibility of Restaurant Partners\. The Company will facilitate the following process:

- Complaint must be raised within 1 \(one\) hour of delivery;
- Clear description and, where possible, photographic evidence of the quality issue must be provided;
- The Company will investigate with the Restaurant Partner and determine whether a refund is warranted;
- Refunds for quality complaints are at the Company's discretion following investigation;
- Repeated complaints against a Restaurant Partner may result in suspension from the Platform\.

__Food Safety Emergency__

If you believe food received has caused or risks causing a food safety or health incident, contact the FSSAI Helpline: 1800\-11\-2100\.

For medical emergencies, seek immediate medical attention first and report to HIVAGO after\.

## __6\.  FAILED PAYMENTS AND TECHNICAL ERRORS__

### __6\.1  Payment Deducted but Order Not Confirmed__

If payment has been deducted from your account but no Order Confirmation was received:

- Do NOT re\-place the order until you have confirmed whether the original payment was deducted;
- Check your bank statement and the HIVAGO Order History screen;
- Email support@hivago\.in with your registered mobile number, date and time of transaction, and bank UTR/reference number;
- Upon PayU confirmation of a failed transaction, a full refund of the deducted amount will be processed within 5–7 Business Days\.

### __6\.2  Double Charge__

If you are charged twice for the same Order due to a payment gateway error:

- Report both transaction references to support@hivago\.in within 48 hours;
- The Company will verify with PayU and process a refund of the duplicate charge within 7 Business Days of confirmation\.

### __6\.3  Company's Liability Limitation__

HIVAGO processes payments exclusively through PayU and does not independently handle payment instruments\. While the Company will fully facilitate investigation and refund processing, it is not liable for errors attributable to PayU's infrastructure, the User's bank, or the card network\. Unresolved payment disputes may need to be escalated directly to PayU and the issuing bank\.

## __8\.  FRAUDULENT AND ABUSIVE REFUND CLAIMS__

8\.1  HIVAGO employs automated and manual fraud detection to identify refund abuse patterns, including:

- Abnormal refund claim frequency from a single account;
- Claims contradicted by delivery GPS records, Delivery Partner logs, or Restaurant Partner packing confirmation;
- Coordinated abuse across multiple accounts linked by device ID, IP address, or payment method;
- Fabricated quality complaints submitted without credible evidence\.

8\.2  Consequences: If the Company determines that a User has submitted fraudulent, exaggerated, or materially false refund claims, it may:

- Reject the refund claim without liability;
- Permanently suspend or terminate the User's account without notice;
- Pursue civil recovery and/or file a criminal complaint under Section 420 of the Indian Penal Code, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and other applicable statutes\.

8\.3  Restaurant Partners and Delivery Partners engaging in fraudulent dispute manipulation will face immediate suspension and potential legal action\.
