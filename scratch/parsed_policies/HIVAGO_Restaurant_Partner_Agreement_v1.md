## __CLAUSE 4  —  MENU LISTINGS, CONTENT, AND PRICING__

### __4\.1  Menu Setup and Management__

The Restaurant Partner shall be responsible for creating, maintaining, and updating its menu listing on the Platform through the Restaurant Partner Dashboard\. The Restaurant Partner shall ensure at all times that:

- All food item names, descriptions, and photographs accurately represent the actual items offered;
- Item availability is kept current — unavailable items must be marked as out\-of\-stock on the Dashboard promptly;
- Prices listed on the Platform correspond exactly to the Restaurant Partner's standard dine\-in or counter prices, with no mark\-up for Platform orders;
- All vegetarian and non\-vegetarian items are clearly identified using the standard FSSAI green dot \(vegetarian\) and brown dot \(non\-vegetarian\) symbols;
- Allergen information, where known, is included in item descriptions;
- Any changes to menu, prices, or availability are updated on the Dashboard before the next service period\.

### __4\.2  No Price Mark\-Up Policy__

__IMPORTANT — PRICING PARITY COMMITMENT__

A core principle of HIVAGO's marketplace is that Users pay the same price for food items as they would at the Restaurant Partner's dine\-in counter\.

The Restaurant Partner expressly agrees that the prices listed on the Platform shall not exceed the Restaurant Partner's standard dine\-in prices for equivalent items\.

Any violation of this pricing parity commitment — including listing items at inflated prices on the Platform — constitutes a material breach of this Agreement and grounds for Suspension\.

HIVAGO reserves the right to conduct random price audits by comparing Platform prices against in\-store menus or published price lists\.

4\.2\.1  The Restaurant Partner may list prices on the Platform that are equal to or lower than its standard dine\-in prices\. Under no circumstances may Platform prices exceed standard dine\-in prices\.

4\.2\.2  The Company may, at its sole discretion, conduct periodic price audits and require the Restaurant Partner to rectify any pricing discrepancy within 24 \(twenty\-four\) hours of notice\.

### __4\.3  Content Ownership and Licence__

4\.3\.1  The Restaurant Partner retains ownership of all Menu Content submitted to the Platform, including food photographs, item descriptions, and branding\. By submitting Menu Content, the Restaurant Partner grants the Company a non\-exclusive, royalty\-free, worldwide licence to use, display, reproduce, and adapt the Menu Content on the Platform and in the Company's marketing and promotional materials for the duration of this Agreement and for a reasonable period thereafter for archival or reference purposes\.

4\.3\.2  The Restaurant Partner warrants that all Menu Content submitted is original, does not infringe any third\-party intellectual property rights, and does not contain any false, misleading, or deceptive information\.

4\.3\.3  The Company reserves the right to remove, modify, or reject any Menu Content that it determines is inaccurate, misleading, in violation of applicable law, or inconsistent with the Platform's community standards, without prior notice to the Restaurant Partner\.

### __5\.1  Order Acceptance and Processing__

5\.1\.1  Upon receipt of an Order notification through the Dashboard, the Restaurant Partner shall accept or reject the Order within 3 \(three\) minutes\. Failure to acknowledge an Order within this window may result in automatic order cancellation\. The Company reserves the right to adjust this acceptance window with notice\.

5\.1\.2  By accepting an Order, the Restaurant Partner enters into a binding contract with the User for the supply of the specified food items at the listed prices\. The Restaurant Partner must fulfil accepted Orders promptly and within the estimated preparation time\.

### __5\.2  Preparation Standards and Food Safety__

The Restaurant Partner is solely and entirely responsible for:

- Preparing, packaging, and ensuring the food safety, hygiene, quality, freshness, and fitness for human consumption of all items in an Order;
- Complying with all FSSAI regulations, food safety guidelines, and hygiene standards applicable to food preparation and packaging;
- Ensuring that food items are packaged securely and appropriately to prevent spillage, contamination, or degradation during delivery;
- Accurately fulfilling Order contents — providing all items, quantities, variants, and customisations as specified in the Order;
- Ensuring that items marked as vegetarian contain no non\-vegetarian ingredients and that allergen information is accurate;
- Maintaining the licensed premises in compliance with all applicable health, sanitation, and local authority requirements\.

__THE COMPANY IS NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR FOOD QUALITY OR SAFETY__

The Company is a technology marketplace intermediary and does not prepare, handle, inspect, or supervise the preparation of any food item\.

All responsibility for food quality, safety, hygiene, ingredient accuracy, allergen content, and fitness for consumption lies exclusively and entirely with the Restaurant Partner\.

The Restaurant Partner agrees to fully indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any and all claims, liabilities, damages, regulatory actions, or penalties arising from food quality, safety, hygiene, or food\-borne illness complaints relating to Orders fulfilled by the Restaurant Partner\.

### __5\.3  Preparation Time Accuracy__

The Restaurant Partner shall set accurate and realistic estimated preparation times on the Dashboard\. Consistently exceeding the stated preparation time adversely affects User experience and may result in poor ratings and, in persistent cases, Platform action\. The Company reserves the right to adjust displayed preparation time estimates based on historical performance data\.

### __5\.4  Order Cancellations by Restaurant Partner__

The Restaurant Partner should not cancel accepted Orders except in genuine operational emergencies\. Restaurant Partner\-initiated cancellations have the following consequences:

- The User is entitled to a full refund \(food value \+ Delivery Fee\) as per the Refund and Cancellation Policy;
- The Restaurant Partner forfeits the settlement for the cancelled Order;
- Repeated cancellations will be monitored\. More than \[3\] cancellations per week or a cancellation rate exceeding \[5%\] of accepted orders in any 30\-day period may result in Suspension or additional review\.

### __5\.5  Delivery Handover__

The Restaurant Partner is responsible for:

- Having the Order ready for collection by the Delivery Partner within the stated preparation time;
- Handing the Order to the assigned Delivery Partner in a properly sealed and complete condition;

### __9\.1  Company's GST Role — Section 9\(5\) CGST__

The Company, as an e\-commerce operator under Section 9\(5\) of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 \('CGST Act'\), is liable to collect and remit GST on restaurant services facilitated through the Platform\. Accordingly:

- The Company issues GST\-compliant tax invoices to Users on each Order, reflecting the Company's GSTIN \(27AAECW3756G1Z3\);
- The Company collects GST on the food value component from Users and remits it to the government;
- The Settlement Amount remitted to the Restaurant Partner is the net amount after GST has been accounted for at the Platform level\.

__Impact of Section 9\(5\) on Restaurant Partners__

Because HIVAGO is liable to remit GST on restaurant services under Section 9\(5\), the tax compliance burden for Platform\-facilitated orders shifts to HIVAGO as the e\-commerce operator\.

Restaurant Partners should consult their chartered accountant or GST advisor to understand how Platform orders interact with their own GST filings and whether any adjustment, credit, or reconciliation is required\.

Restaurant Partners are advised to disclose their HIVAGO listing and earnings to their GST advisor for accurate tax filing\.

### __9\.2  Restaurant Partner's Independent GST Obligations__

The Company's discharge of GST obligations under Section 9\(5\) applies only to food supply services facilitated through the Platform\. The Restaurant Partner remains independently responsible for:

- Maintaining proper books of accounts and GST records for all sales, whether on\-Platform or off\-Platform;
- Paying its own GST liability on non\-Platform sales and other taxable supplies;
- Complying with all other obligations under the CGST Act, IGST Act, and applicable State GST laws\.

9\.3  The Company is not responsible for the Restaurant Partner's individual GST compliance obligations and shall not be held liable for any penalties, interest, or assessments arising from the Restaurant Partner's failure to comply with its GST obligations\.
